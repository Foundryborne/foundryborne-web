Available Modules

Core Rulebook

The Daggerheart Core Rulebook module for Foundry Virtual Tabletop contains sumptuously stylized journals of the complete rules text, vibrant art for all ancestry, domain, class, and subclass cards.

  • Book Inspired Journals: Beautifully styled journals for the rules text, cross-linked for easy reference.
  • Complete Card Collection: Want to play a mushroom-like Fungril? Or explore you demon heritage as an Inferis? Bring them to life with full original art for all ancestries from humans to halflings. Art and full text for community, class, and subclass cards will help you develop your characters as they adventure, so you can try out the Call of the Slayer to strike down adversaries with immense force or Warden of Renewal to use powerful magic to heal your party. Don’t forget the Domain cards! Arcana to Valor are all here for you to combine to craft your abilities and spells.
  • Tokens & Art: Static and Dynamic tokens for adversaries and ancestries to bring your challenges to life.
  • Robust Campaign Frame Support: Detailed additional rules and content required to use the entire set of core Campaign Frames.
  • Gentle Guidance for GMs & Players: Learn from the designers to improve your campaign building and enhance player experiences with their advice and best practices and Player Principles for use in the world of Daggerheart.

Hope & Fear

Daggerheart: Hope & Fear offers a wealth of options for both players and GMs to expand the way they play Daggerheart. 130+ new adversaries, 28 environments, and a ready-to-go rip pad of blank adversary sheets for when inspiration strikes. Plus, Four new campaign frames introduce wholly different genres, mechanics, themes, and tools to surprise and delight their players!

  • Stylized Journals: Beautifully styled journals for the rules text, cross-linked for easy reference.
  • Card & Token Art: Art for all ancestry, domain, class, and subclass cards along with artwork and tokens for adversaries, including the awaited Dread Domain and its ghastly, macabre magic core.
  • New Classes with Additional Subclasses: Witch, Warlock, Brawler, and Assassin for you to create talismans, weave illusions, seek vengeance, develop body and mind to work as one, or inflict precise strikes. Players will revel in the over one-hundred new items, weapon, and armor options, as well as the new player classes! Including the 12 new Domains and Ancestries - Duneborne, Hearthborne, Frostborne, Warborne, Freeborne, and Reborne along with Skykin, Tidekin, Earthkin, Emberkin, Gnome, and Aetheris.
  • Transformation Cards: A completely new mechanic, where 6 transformations represent changes or augmentations to characters in Daggerheart. Transform into a Vampire, Werewolf, Reanimated, Shapeshifter, Ghost, or Demigod!
  • New Campaign Frame Support: Additional rules and content provided for the new worlds and settings for you to explore.
  • Expanded Dynamic Adversaries & Environments: More than 130 new adversaries and 28 environments to challenge your heroes, complete with art and rules automation for easy use in your campaign.
This module requires the Dagger Heart Core Rulebook module to use.

Daggerheart Duality Dice

The Daggerheart Duality Dice Set is a collection of four unique 3d dice sets specially designed for use with the Daggerheart system and the Dice so Nice module.

  • Set one features a new 3d model for rhombic d12s and custom animations to truly differentiate Hope and Fear dice.
  • Sets two, three, and four contain unique contrasting color designs with additional d12 designs for rolling Hope and Fear.
  • This module requires Dice so Nice to function.
  • It does not require the Daggerheart system but some features are only available with Daggerheart installed.
